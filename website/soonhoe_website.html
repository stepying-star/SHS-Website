Who We Are

Company Introduction

SOON HOE MANAGEMENT PTE LTD

Soon Hoe Management Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based professional services firm providing accounting, audit, tax, corporate secretarial, HR and administration services, together with strategic consulting. We help companies stay compliant, run efficiently and grow with confidence.

For business owners and families from Southeast Asia and Greater China, we also offer bespoke advisory on residency planning, overseas asset allocation, children's education, family office matters and property — coordinating each step with trusted, licensed partners where required. Through our AI brand GAIAGenX, we also help companies put AI to work in their daily operations.

"Steady, discreet support — for your business today and your family's next chapter."
